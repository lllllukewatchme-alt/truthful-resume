# General content template

Default section order for general business roles: summary → experience → projects → education → skills.
Used when no more specific content template exists. Fresh graduates: put `education` before `experience`
(set `section_order` in the resume front matter).

Guidance
- 3-6 bullets per role, most recent role first; each bullet = action + scope + result (only facts the user supplied).
- Keep the summary to 2-4 lines; do not invent adjectives that cannot be evidenced.
